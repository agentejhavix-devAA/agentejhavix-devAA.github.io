The regulation

Four dates that decide when you file

Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 bans products linked to deforestation or forest degradation from the EU market. Operators and traders must prove their supply chains are deforestation-free — plot by plot — before the products can be placed on the market.

since 31.12.2020

Cut-off date

Products are compliant only if the land producing the commodity was not deforested or degraded after 31 December 2020.

FAQ

What operators are asking right now

Is EUDR already in force?
The Regulation has been in force since 2023, but the obligations start on 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators/traders and 30 June 2027 for micro and small undertakings (dates postponed once more in 2026). The EU Information System for due-diligence statements is live. [1][2][3]
Which products are covered?
Seven commodities and their derived products (Annex I of the Regulation): cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soy and wood — including processed goods such as chocolate, leather, tyres, furniture or paper containing them. [1][2]
What must I actually do?
Before placing covered products on the EU market: carry out due diligence (identify the commodity, geolocate the plots of origin with coordinates to 4 decimal places, verify no deforestation after 31 December 2020), assess and mitigate risk, and file a due-diligence statement in the EU Information System. [1][3]
What are the penalties?
Under Article 25, member states must set penalties including a maximum fine of at least 4% of the company's annual EU-wide turnover, confiscation of products and revenue, prohibition to place products on the market, and temporary exclusion from public procurement. [4]
Operator or trader — what's the difference?
An operator is the first to place covered products on the EU market; a trader makes them available in the course of commercial activity. Both must file due-diligence statements; micro and small undertakings benefit from simplified rules. [1][2]
Where do I file the due-diligence statement?
In the EU Information System for deforestation-free products (TRACES). You must register before your first submission, and each DDS gets a reference number to share with customers and keep for five years. [1][3]
