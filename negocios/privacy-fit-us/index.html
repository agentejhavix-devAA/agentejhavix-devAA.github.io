The rule

Same idea, 20 different thresholds

Most laws kick in at 100,000 residents' data — but not all. Getting this wrong is expensive: California alone fines up to $7,988 per intentional violation, and it adds up per consumer.

Common

Most states (Virginia, Colorado, Indiana, Kentucky…) apply once you process the data of 100,000+ residents — or fewer if you sell personal data.

Broadest

Texas has no revenue threshold: it applies to almost any business operating in Texas that isn't an SBA-defined "small business". Rhode Island triggers at just 35,000 residents.

Penalties

California (CCPA): up to $2,663 per unintentional and $7,988 per intentional violation (2026, inflation-adjusted). Enforced by the state AG and the CPPA.
